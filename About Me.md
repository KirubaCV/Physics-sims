## How I Work
Not every problem here is solved purely from my own brain. If a problem defeats me, I take it to the Physics Forums, work through it with their help, and always give explicit credit in my posts. I believe in being honest about what I know and what I'm still learning.

Expect 1–2 hard physics problems per week, occasional Python simulations, and a lot of equations. If you find a mistake, please let me know—I'm always learning.
